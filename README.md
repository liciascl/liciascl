I'm a PhD student at UFABC. My research involves the development and evaluation of non-invasive biosensors based on radiofrequency (RF) and microwave technologies. It investigates the interaction between electromagnetic signals and biological tissues for physiological monitoring, with a particular focus on non-invasive glucose sensing. My research also involves the use of High-Performance Computing (HPC) to accelerate the simulation of these systems.

I work as a Professor at Insper, teaching undergraduate courses in High-Performance Computing  and Programming. I have over 7 years of experience as a technician in Insper's laboratories, specializing in Robotics, Artificial Intelligence, Computer Vision, Parallel Programming, Distributed Programming, HPC, Robot Operating System (ROS), Python (Programming Language), Raspberry Pi, Shell Scripting, and GNU/Linux.
